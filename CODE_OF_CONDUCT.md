This is an original short policy written for this repository. It is not the
Contributor Covenant and should not be cited as it.

---

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other interaction in
this repository.

## Expected conduct

- Argue with the guide, not with the person. Disagreement about a tier
  definition, a stop rule or a citation is the point of a public reference.
- Bring evidence. A correction that quotes the passage and cites a primary
  source can be acted on. An assertion cannot.
- Accept that this is a maintained reference, not a consensus document. A
  well-argued change may still be declined because it does not fit the guide's
  scope or structure. The reason will be given.
- Respect the confidentiality rules in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md). Do not post
  client material, real organisation detail, or anything traceable to a real
  engagement, including your own employer's, unless you are authorised to
  publish it.

## Unacceptable conduct

- Personal attacks, harassment, or demeaning comments about a person or group.
- Discriminatory language, including in examples submitted for inclusion.
- Posting someone else's private or identifying information.
- Sustained disruption of discussion, including repeatedly reopening a settled
  question without new evidence.

## Reporting and enforcement

Raise a concern by opening an issue, or privately through the contact route on
the maintainer's GitHub profile if the matter should not be public.

This repository is maintained by one practitioner. Reports are handled by the
maintainer, who will act proportionately: a request to change behaviour, the
editing or removal of a comment, or blocking a participant from the repository
where conduct continues or is severe. There is no appeal body. If you are the
subject of a decision you think is wrong, say so and it will be reconsidered
once.
